# Matrix

**Matrix** is a gameplay-expanding mod for *Minecraft*, designed to weave together a variety of creative and
interesting, like a matrix of ideas, into a rich gameplay experience.

## Gameplay Features

Wizard - Series of powerful items that grant players the ability to wield magic and manipulate arcane forces.

**Lightning** - Armor that bends space and time, empowering players in both mobility and combat.

**Wither Suit** - They went all out, leaving no stone unturned, and spared no effort in protecting its owner.

- **Sculk** - Grants players extreme combat prowess, channeling the relentless strength of the Warden itself.
-
- **Ore Tools and Armors** - New tools and armors crafted from various ores, each with unique abilities and functional
  traits.

- **Generic Items** - A collection of standalone items that are useful, interesting, and sometimes surprising.

- **Enchantments** - Enhance your gear with new enchantments that synergize with Matrix armors, weapons, and tools.
